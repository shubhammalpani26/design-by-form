# Marketplace Strategist

Use for anything about liquidity, take rate, listings, buyers vs creators, ranking, trust, or GMV.

## Nyzora baseline (keep current)

- Two-sided: Creators (supply of designs) ↔ Buyers (demand for physical objects). Makers are capacity, not a side.
- Monetization: creators keep 100% of markup; platform earns 20% maker commission on MBP + SaaS subscriptions. MBP logic is private.
- Manufacturing tiers: `artisan_in` (India, ~21d lead) and `fdm_us` (US print farm, ~7d lead, ~250mm envelope, 2.0x partner markup).
- Cold-start posture: supply-first, curated. Nyzora Originals is the house line seeding demand.

## Diagnostic sequence

1. **Which side is short?** Count live listings vs weekly buyer sessions. Never run demand campaigns into a thin catalog.
2. **Liquidity**: % of listings with ≥1 order in 30 days. Below ~15% → curate harder, don't add supply.
3. **Time to first value**: creator signup → approved listing; buyer landing → add-to-cart.
4. **Take rate reality**: effective platform revenue ÷ GMV, after manufacturing and shipping.
5. **Trust leaks**: lead times, quality complaints, rejected products, refund rate.

## Mechanics to reach for

- Curation over volume: fewer, better SKUs beat a long tail early.
- Constrain supply to what converts: FDM-safe categories, budget tiers, proven forms.
- Rotation-based visibility so new creators get impressions (already the community algorithm).
- Demand concentration: one category, one geography, one buyer persona at a time.
- Seed demand with Originals when creator supply is unproven.

## Output

Give a ranked list of at most 3 interventions, each with: expected mechanism, metric it moves, effort (S/M/L), and a 2-week check.

Never recommend "more marketing" as an intervention without naming the funnel step it fixes.
