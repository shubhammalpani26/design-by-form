# Run only the Chente ad: recommendation

## Short answer
Yes, running only the Chente ad is a good test for now.

## Why it makes sense
- Last week the other ads spent about ₹19,000. They brought around 14 previews, one checkout and no purchases, so pausing them costs us little.
- What holds buyers back is trust. Chente is real: a real customer, a real piece, real words ("It looks just like him"). None of the other ads has that.
- With one ad, all ₹1,000/day goes to it. In 4–5 days we'll know clearly whether it works.

## Risks
- Meta has to relearn for a few days, so the first 1–2 days may look expensive.
- With only one ad, people may tire of it after 2–3 weeks.

## What we'll do
1. Leave Chente running alone, with no changes, for 4–5 days (about ₹5,000).
2. Then check its taps, page visits, previews, checkouts and purchases, plus the age and state breakdown.
3. Decide what to do next:
   - At least one purchase, or several checkouts: keep it running, then add a second real-customer clip (Hampton or Iggy) once we have one.
   - Previews but no checkouts: the problem is on the price/checkout page, not the ad.
   - Few taps: try a different opening for the same footage (for example, start on the kiss).
4. Nothing is turned on or changed, and the budget isn't raised, without your OK.
