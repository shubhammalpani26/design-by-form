# Human Memorial Busts (future category, not yet built)

## The thesis
Today grief is displayed as a **flat photo in a frame**. The expansion is to replace the framed photo
with a sculpted bust of the person — parent, grandparent, spouse — made from the same
photo-to-piece pipeline already running for pets.

Positioning is deliberately **memorial, not celebratory**. Existing players (Doob, Hologram-style
mini-me scanners, wedding cake toppers) sell the fun, alive, novelty miniature. We sell the
remembrance object that sits on a mantel next to a candle. Different buyer, different moment,
different price tolerance.

## Why it fits Nyzora
- Same stack: Gemini photo → bust render → Meshy mesh → partner slice/quote → US print, one solid part.
- Same emotional trigger and same ad creative logic as pet memorials (loss, anniversary, first
  holiday without them).
- Higher AOV justified: a human memorial is a once-in-a-lifetime purchase, not a gift impulse.
- Same size ladder (petite/standard/statement) and the same engraved plinth (name + dates).

## What must be true before launch
1. **Likeness bar is far higher than pets.** A dog that is 85% right reads as the dog. A grandmother
   at 85% reads as *wrong* and becomes upsetting. Needs a stricter likeness gate — probably a
   human-in-the-loop approval on the render before it goes to print, at least for the first N orders.
2. **Tone and copy discipline.** No "fun", no "3D printed you", no novelty language. Quiet,
   funeral-adjacent register. Copy closer to a headstone engraver than a gift shop.
3. **Refund/remake policy needs to be softer here** than made-to-order pet policy — a likeness
   dispute on a deceased parent cannot be argued with. Assume free remake, generous.
4. **Channel differs.** Pet memorial is Meta interest targeting. Human memorial is partly search
   intent ("memorial gift for loss of mother", "cremation urn alternative") and potentially
   funeral-home / celebrant partnerships — a real B2B2C wedge.

## Sequencing
Do not launch until pet memorial is proven on paid orders/30d. Human memorial is the *upgrade* of
the same machine, and it should inherit a working funnel rather than test two unknowns at once.
